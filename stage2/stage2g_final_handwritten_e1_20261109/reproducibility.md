Architecture, vocabulary, normalization, aspect-preserving height 64 resize, true CTC lengths, greedy decoding and edit metrics come directly from Stage 2E. No width cap, truncation, label edits or augmentations are used. Leading ASCII spaces are stripped according to manifest ocr_text; internal whitespace remains intact. No-whitespace CER removes all whitespace on both sides. Exact-tier accuracy is space sensitive. Edit counts follow the original deterministic alignment tie rules.

One pilot: seed 42, two full handwritten epochs, AdamW learning rate 1e-5, weight decay 1e-4, gradient clip 1, frozen BatchNorm running statistics, affine trainable. Each update uses two handwritten and two replay tiers, except each epoch's last update uses one handwritten and two replay tiers; losses are averaged over the actual batch. Handwritten samples are shuffled each epoch with a seeded local RNG; replay cycles through a separately shuffled training-only list. training_exposures.csv records actual domain and sample exposures. Candidate checkpoints retain E1 vocabulary metadata plus optimizer/config and RNG state. Reproduction is from the beginning; automatic resume is not implemented.

Selection: minimum validation CER including spaces, tie broken by CER excluding whitespace. Regression is evaluated on all existing clean historical (645), clean validation (63), and positive-edge development (431) tiers. Regression inputs and replay have disjoint source parents. Scanned exploratory and protected-test cohorts are never evaluated. Readiness requires handwritten improvement and CER increases no greater than 0.001 absolute on both clean cohorts and 0.005 on positive development. No wider hyperparameter search.
