# Augmentation quality

Accepted 200 random examples; 77 source attempts exhausted rejection sampling and were resampled. Contact sheets pair source and augmented crops. All checks operate at model height 64.

Small compact detached components are processed separately from skeletonized body ink. Every component must map one-to-one after transformation; total component counts and body holes must match. Hollow/filled detached marks are allowed. This conservative automatic guard is not semantic reader verification; connected dots cannot be independently identified without glyph annotation. Unconfirmed X/7/slanted-bar substitutions are disabled and never silently mixed.

Requested stroke widths 1.5–4 and grey ink 30–200 are sampled; actual distributions are measured, not assumed. Rejection sampling changes the accepted parameter distribution. Whole-tier pitch and scale are used; independent glyph/column annotation is unavailable.
